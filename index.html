    About the Book of Remembrance

    St Leonard's Church, Woodcote maintains a Book of Remembrance containing the names of those remembered on the anniversary of their passing.

    The Book records only those who are interred in the churchyard Garden of Remembrance.

    This digital version shows each page of the Book on its day of remembrance, and lets you browse the Book at any time of year.
